RAG Architecture

Custom RAG vs. Legacy Rule-Based Chatbots

Legacy chatbots (like older Intercom or Drift setups) rely on rigid conditional "if-this-then-that" decision trees. If a prospect asks an unscripted question, the bot fails. Shebercraft builds autonomous RAG agents utilizing high-dimensional vector embeddings (Pinecone) over your proprietary documentation. The LLM synthesizes context-aware, human-level answers within strict guardrails, eliminating support dead ends.

Revenue Operations

How AI SDRs Double Inbound Conversion Rates

67% of B2B buyer inquiries occur outside typical 9-to-5 working hours. While traditional web forms make prospects wait 12 to 24 hours for a sales rep email, Shebercraft's 24/7 Autonomous SDR responds in under 3 seconds. It qualifies lead budget, timeline, and company size (BANT scoring) and immediately books discovery calls directly into Google Calendar or Calendly.

Zero-Hallucination SLA

Enterprise Privacy & Semantic Grounding

Corporate compliance demands 100% accuracy. Our agents are built with strict cosine-similarity thresholds: if the user's inquiry cannot be verified with a 90%+ confidence score against your indexed documentation, the model gracefully declines to speculate and seamlessly routes the conversation to your designated human account executive on Slack.

How Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Works

Traditional SEO targets 10 blue links on Google. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) structures site entities, Linked Data graphs, and factual semantic clusters so that AI engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, and Google AI Overviews) synthesize and recommend your company as the authoritative answer for commercial queries in your niche.

/ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know
Before Starting Collaboration

What is an autonomous AI agent for B2B companies?

An autonomous AI agent is an intelligent software employee built on large language models (OpenAI GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet) connected to your company's private documentation via vector RAG. Unlike simple chatbots with static decision trees, it autonomously qualifies prospective buyers, handles objections with accurate company data, checks real-time calendar availability, books discovery meetings, and logs structured deals into HubSpot or Salesforce.

How much does it cost to build a custom AI agent?

At Shebercraft, a custom autonomous AI concierge starts at a fixed investment of $2,490. This includes RAG vector database indexing of private company whitepapers and FAQs, Calendly/CRM integrations, multi-channel deployment (Web + SMS), guardrails to eliminate hallucinations, and 45 days of continuous prompt tuning. Full-stack enterprise deployments start at $4,900.

How does Shebercraft eliminate AI hallucinations?

We implement strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures with semantic vector similarity scoring. When a user asks a question, the agent retrieves only verified passages from your private knowledge base and is mathematically restricted from inventing facts. If an answer cannot be found in your documentation, the agent politely admits it and routes the prospect to a human specialist.

Do we own 100% of the code and intellectual property?

Yes, absolutely. Upon final milestone payment, full ownership of all source code, Figma design assets, prompt engineering architectures, and intellectual property transfers entirely to your company under our standard IP agreement with zero vendor lock-in.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the evolution of SEO for AI search engines. It optimizes your digital footprint so that leading generative engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Claude, Google AI Overviews) cite and recommend your brand as the authoritative solution when prospective buyers search for your services.

How do your AI agents integrate with HubSpot and Salesforce?

Our AI agents communicate via secure bi-directional REST APIs and webhooks. When a qualified prospect converses with the AI, the agent automatically creates or updates the CRM contact, scores lead intent, attaches the full conversation summary, and triggers Slack notifications to the appropriate sales rep.

What is the typical turnaround time for a project?

We work in agile 7–14 day engineering sprints. High-converting B2B landing pages are delivered in 7 business days. Custom autonomous AI concierges are deployed in 10–14 business days. Corporate web applications take 14–21 business days.
